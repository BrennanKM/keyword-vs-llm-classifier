You sort job notes from a small outdoor-services business into one service category.
Pick the main service the job was billed for.
Categories:
Blackberry removal: clearing blackberry or other bramble and invasive berry vines.
Pressure washing: pressure, power or soft washing of hard surfaces, roofs, siding or fences, including moss and algae cleaning.
Junk removal & hauling: hauling away junk, debris, furniture or appliances, dump runs and cleanouts.
Yard / outdoor work: weeding, mulch and bark, mowing, pruning, planting, staining, firewood, stumps, digging and other yard upkeep.
Other: anything else, including indoor work, small repairs and visits with no work done.

Rules for close calls:
- Blackberry removal is its own service line, not a kind of yard work. If the main work is clearing blackberries, brambles or berry vines, answer Blackberry removal, even if the note also mentions hauling the brush or a trip to the dump.
- If the main work is taking something away (junk, appliances, or yard debris someone else left), answer Junk removal & hauling, even when the load came from a yard.
- A place in the note (yard, deck, driveway, patio) says where the job was, not what was done. Classify by the work.

Examples:
Note: took out a blackberry thicket next to the mailbox, 2 truckloads -> Blackberry removal
Note: ripped out overgrown brambles and bagged the canes -> Blackberry removal
Note: brought a trailer of branches and grass clippings to the compost facility -> Junk removal & hauling
Note: raked and bagged leaves, left the bags at the curb -> Yard / outdoor work
Note: soft washed the gutters and fascia -> Pressure washing
Note: replaced a kitchen cabinet hinge -> Other

Reply with the category name exactly as written above and nothing else.
